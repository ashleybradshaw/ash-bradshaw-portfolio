## THE PRODUCT CHALLENGE

A tight six-week window to ship three regional microsites and two lookbooks, each needing to feel intensely local without fracturing the overarching campaign system.

## SYSTEM & EXECUTION

Instead of brute-forcing three separate rebuilds, the lookbooks and microsites were architected as a single, modular kit.

A shared grid, typography, and motion language carried the ZOKU story, with regional swaps isolated purely to content, imagery, and localisation rather than underlying page structure.

## IMPACT

- Three regional microsites and two digital lookbooks produced from the exact same component system.
- Delivered seamlessly inside a highly compressed six-week window.
- Campaign language held together securely across the UK, USA, and Australia markets.
